These three commands make new shapes from existing ones. Each is one undo step. They work on the
2D profiles only: they do not change Z ranges, and the new features keep the role of the shapes
they came from.

## Before you start

- Open the **Sketch** view and select the features to work on. Locked features are left out.
- Text and imported models cannot be offset or joined. To change the outline of text, expand it
  first; see [Text](/guide/design/text/#expand-text).

## Offset


<VisualPending id="shape-offset-preview" />
**Create offset feature** makes a new outline at a fixed distance inside or outside the selected
closed features. The originals stay as they are.

<Steps>

1. Select one or more closed features, then click **Create offset feature** in the tool rail.

2. At **Preview distance**, move the pointer inside or outside a feature. The preview follows the
   pointer (**Move inside or outside the feature to preview. Click to commit.**).

3. Click to create the offset at the previewed distance. Or click **Distance** (<kbd>Tab</kbd>),
   type the distance, and click **Confirm**: a negative distance offsets inwards, a positive one
   outwards. The field starts with the distance under the pointer.

</Steps>

When several features are selected, they are offset as one combined outline: offsets of shapes that
come close to each other merge. The new features are selected afterwards.

## Join

**Join closed features** merges overlapping closed shapes into one.

<Steps>

1. Select two or more closed features that overlap or share an edge, then click **Join closed
   features**.

2. If the selection is unambiguous, PureCut CNC joins it straight away. Otherwise the **Join** panel
   opens at **Select features**: click shapes to add them to the join, and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click
   one to remove it. Only shapes that overlap or share an edge with the others can be added. The
   panel counts the closed features selected; a join needs at least two.

3. Set **Keep originals** (<kbd>K</kbd>) if you want to keep the separate shapes too, then click
   **Confirm**.

</Steps>

The join happens without the panel only when every selected feature is closed, unlocked, not text
or a model, has the same role, and they all form one connected group. Shapes that touch only at a
corner are not connected.

The joined feature takes the role of the first selected feature. Without **Keep originals**, it
replaces the shapes that were joined.

To connect two open paths end to end, use sketch editing instead: drag an endpoint onto the other
path's endpoint. See [Editing sketches](/guide/design/sketch-editing/#join-open-paths).

## Cut


<VisualPending id="shape-cut-workflow" />
**Cut features** removes the area of one or more shapes, the cutters, from others, the targets, or
splits the targets along open paths.

<Steps>

1. Click **Cut features**. If exactly one feature is selected, it becomes the cutter and the panel
   goes straight to step 3.

2. At **Select cutters**, click the shapes to cut with, then click **Next** (<kbd>Tab</kbd>).

3. At **Select targets**, click the shapes to cut.

4. Set **Keep originals** (<kbd>K</kbd>) if you want to keep the uncut targets, then click
   **Confirm**.

</Steps>

| Cutter | Closed target | Open target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed | The cutter's area is removed; the rest may fall into several pieces. | The part of the path inside the cutter is removed. |
| Open | The target is split into pieces wherever the path crosses it completely. A path that does not cross it completely leaves it unchanged. | No change. |

The pieces keep the target's role, Z range, and folder, and take its place in the feature tree
unless **Keep originals** is on. The cutters are never removed.
