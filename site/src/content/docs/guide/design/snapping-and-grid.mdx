Snapping pulls the pointer to exact positions while you draw, move, measure, or edit: grid points,
corners, midpoints, centres, and intersections. It applies to every click a command asks for, not
just to drawing.

## Snap modes


<span id="modes"></span>

The snapping controls are in the top toolbar: one button turns snapping on or off
(<AppIcon name="snap" /> **Enable snapping** / **Disable snapping**), and one button per mode turns
that mode on or off. On a tablet the same controls are in the **Snap settings** popover.

<Screenshot src={snapModes} alt="Toolbar snap buttons with snapping, grid, point, line, midpoint, and center on and intersection and perpendicular off, and a Midpoint label at the pointer on a rectangle's top edge" caption="A label next to the pointer names the snap that will be used." />

| Mode | Snaps to | On by default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <AppIcon name="snap-grid" /> **Snap to grid** | The nearest multiple of the grid's **Snap increment**. | Yes |
| <AppIcon name="snap-point" /> **Snap to point** | Corners and end points of features, the stock, tabs, and clamps, and the origin. | Yes |
| <AppIcon name="snap-line" /> **Snap to line** | The nearest point on any segment. | Yes |
| <AppIcon name="snap-midpoint" /> **Snap to midpoint** | The middle of segments. | Yes |
| <AppIcon name="snap-center" /> **Snap to center** | The centre of circles and arcs. | Yes |
| <AppIcon name="snap-intersection" /> **Snap to intersection** | Points where two segments cross, even where there is no corner. | No |
| <AppIcon name="snap-perpendicular" /> **Snap perpendicular** | The point on a segment that makes a right angle with the previous point of the command. | No |

Snapping considers visible features, including construction geometry and regions, and visible tabs
and clamps. When the pointer is within reach of several candidates, a point, midpoint, centre, or
intersection wins over a perpendicular point, which wins over a point on a line, which wins over the
grid. A small label next to the pointer names the snap that will be used, so you can check it before
you click.

Your snap settings are a preference of the app on this device, not part of the project.

## Grid

Select **Grid** in the feature tree to change the grid:

| Setting | Description | Default (inch / mm) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Grid extent** | The size of the drawn grid. | 8 in / 200 mm |
| **Major lines** | The spacing of the heavier grid lines. | 1 in / 10 mm |
| **Minor lines** | The spacing of the lighter lines between them. | 0.25 in / 2 mm |
| **Snap increment** | The step that **Snap to grid** uses. It can be finer than the minor lines. | 0.125 in / 1 mm |
| **Visible** | Shows or hides the grid. Grid snapping still works while it is hidden. | On |

The **Grid** toggle in the status bar also shows or hides the grid. Grid settings are saved with the
project.

## Axis lock

The axis lock keeps a point on a horizontal or vertical line through the point you started from,
while you draw, move, or drag points.

- Press <kbd>Alt</kbd> (<kbd>Option</kbd> on macOS) to cycle the lock: first **Lock X**, then
  **Lock Y**, then off.
- While a lock is on, a **Lock X** or **Lock Y** chip shows on the canvas; click it to cycle.
- On a tablet, use the **Lock** button.

The lock is released when the command ends.
