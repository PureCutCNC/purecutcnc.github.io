Open the **3D** tab to examine the project as a solid. It is especially useful for checking depth, open features, imported models, and the relationship between the cutter path and the stock.

## Navigation


<VisualPending id="view-3d-camera-menu" />
Drag to orbit, use the wheel or trackpad to zoom, and pan with the camera controls. Open the camera menu to choose a view preset, including isometric and orthographic directions such as top, front, and right. Use **Reset view** in that menu to return to the default isometric view; double-clicking the viewport does not reset the camera.

## Model

The 3D view combines visible project features into the model inside the stock. Use it to check that additive and subtractive features have the depth and orientation you intended. It is a preview of the project geometry, not a substitute for checking tooling, workholding, and generated motion.

## Toolpaths

Toolpath overlays use the same display choices as the Sketch view. Open **Show** to isolate cuts, rapids, plunges, retractions, lead-ins, directions, or feed colours. Use the [Toolpath preview](/guide/verify-export/toolpath-preview/) page for the legend and level filter details.

## Open features

Open features are shown where a shape does not form a closed solid boundary. Inspect these carefully: a surface that looks open may not produce the solid or machining result you expect.

## Imported models

Imported mesh models appear in the 3D view with the project geometry. They can help you position design work and inspect a combined scene. Their visibility and inclusion in STL export are controlled separately in **Export model**.
