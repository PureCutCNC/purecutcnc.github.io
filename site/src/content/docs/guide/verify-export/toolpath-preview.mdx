Use the toolpath preview in the Sketch and 3D views to check the moves PureCutCNC has generated. It is a visual aid: use it with [Simulation](/guide/verify-export/simulation/) and the operation warnings before you cut.

## Toolpath legend

<Availability kind="preview" />

The legend starts with **Cuts**. Renderer controls are in the generation gear menu's
**Display** section.

<span id="legend"></span>

Open the **Show** control in the viewport corner to choose which overlays are visible. You can independently show **Cuts**, **Lead-ins**, **Rapids**, **Plunges**, **Retractions**, and **Directions**. The same visibility choices are shared by the Sketch and 3D views. An amber overlay calls attention to a clamp collision.

<Screenshot maxWidth={710} src={toolpathLegend} alt="The expanded legend across the top of the viewport: the 2D renderer control, the vertical Toolpath level rail set to All, and toggles for Cuts, Lead-ins, Rapids, Plunges, Retractions, Directions, and Feed colours" />

## Feed colours

Turn on **Feed colours** in the legend to colour cutting moves by their programmed feed percentage. The **Feed percentage** key explains the colour scale. It is a quick way to find feed reductions; it does not prove that a feed is safe for your material, cutter, or machine.

<span id="collapse"></span>

## Levels

For **2.5D operations**, use the vertical **Toolpath level** rail to isolate a cutting Z level. Choose **All** to restore every level. The same level filter is available in the [3D view](/guide/verify-export/3d-view/), so you can inspect that one pass from another angle. This filter changes only the preview, not the generated toolpath or exported G-code.

<Screenshot src={levelRail} alt="An outside edge route with the level rail moved off All to 0.125, so the preview draws that one cutting depth instead of every pass" caption="The rail reads from the top down; its value shows the Z it is holding." />

## Renderer

<Availability kind="preview" />

Open the generation gear menu in the operations panel. Under **Display**, **2D renderer**
switches between **GPU** and **Canvas**. GPU is the default for the Sketch toolpath overlay;
a saved choice is kept. Unavailable WebGL2 or software-only WebGL falls back to Canvas with
**GPU unavailable; using Canvas.** Use **Retry GPU** after resolving the underlying problem.
The legend itself has no GPU control.

## Warnings

Read warnings in the operation properties and in the export dialog before relying on a preview. A warning identifies something to inspect; it does not necessarily prevent export. Errors are shown separately and can disable export until the underlying problem is corrected. See [Warnings and messages](/guide/reference/warnings/) when that reference page is available.

## Previewing both setups

<Availability kind="preview" />

Visible Top and Bottom toolpaths are shown together in the stock. Paths from the other setup
are faint beneath those of the active setup; a selected operation is drawn at full strength.
Switch face to inspect the other side's work, or use the eye on a setup's header in the
Operations panel to show or hide only that setup's toolpaths. The display does not combine the programs into
one machining run. See [Setups and faces](/guide/fundamentals/setups-and-faces/).

## Plasma kerf and pierces

<Availability kind="experimental" />

A **Plasma through-cut** path has a translucent band one **Kerf width** wide below its centre
line in Sketch and 3D. It lies on the sheet top. The contour is in the cuts colour; lead-in
and lead-out bands use the plunges colour. Small fixed-size rings mark pierces.

**Cuts** toggles the contour band, **Lead-ins** toggles the lead band, and **Plunges** toggles
the pierce markers. Inspect the retained side, lead clearance, and pierce location as well as
the centre line. The ring marks a location, not the crater's physical size. See
[Plasma through-cut](/guide/operations/plasma-through-cut/#verifying-the-result) for the planned
kerf-and-pierce visual and [Simulation](/guide/verify-export/simulation/#simulating-plasma)
for material removal.
