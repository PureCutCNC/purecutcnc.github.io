The exported motion inspector is a diagnostic view for comparing the path generated by PureCut CNC with the optimized moves and the motion parsed from the prepared G-code.

## Opening

Open **Export G-code**, select exactly one eligible planar operation, and choose **Inspect exported motion**. The button does not depend on the development-only **Debug toolpath** checkbox. It appears only after preparation succeeds for one operation whose motion can be represented as a flat plan trace; drilling, operations with no cuts, and continuously varying-Z motion are not eligible.

<Screenshot src={motionInspector} alt="Exported motion inspector comparing motion layers" />

## Layers

Use the layer checkboxes to compare **Generated (raw)**, **Optimized**, and **Exported G-code** motion. You can also show **Non-cutting moves**, choose a **Cutting level**, and choose **All cutting levels**. Pan, zoom, select a zoom region, or use **Fit** to inspect a location.

## Counts

The inspector reports **Raw moves**, **Optimized moves**, and **Removed**, plus emitted **Linear**, **Rapid**, **CW arcs**, and **CCW arcs** counts. These numbers help explain the effect of the linear optimizer and the postprocessor; they are not a cycle-time prediction.

## Verification

When the inspector reports **Verified — exported motion matches the generated path within tolerance.**, the comparison succeeded within its geometric tolerance. A warning identifies a difference or condition that needs investigation before you rely on the program.

## Arc fitting

Arc fitting is an operation output setting. It can emit validated G2/G3 arcs when the active machine supports the required arc format. Review the generated program and the controller’s documented capabilities before using it in production.

## Arc validation

The export path validates arc output against the selected machine definition. If an arc cannot be represented safely for that definition, follow the export message rather than assuming every controller interprets the same syntax.

## Optimizer

PureCut CNC applies the linear-move optimizer automatically. The inspector makes its effect visible by showing raw and optimized move counts alongside the exported motion.
