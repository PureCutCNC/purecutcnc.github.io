**3D surface rough** removes bulk material from a model in horizontal levels. Use it before 3D
surface finish when the model needs a controlled roughing pass rather than a 2.5D pocket.

<Screenshot src={roughLevels} alt="3D surface rough cutting paths around the Busto head, neck, and base" caption="On the Busto model, roughing clears material at successive levels while leaving stock for the finish pass." />

<Screenshot src={roughResult} alt="Simulated Busto stock after roughing, with stepped levels around the head and face" caption="The rough simulation shows the bust emerging in steps, before the surface finish pass." />

## Requirements

| Requirement | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Target features | 3D model features; regions can limit the cut |
| Tool | Flat or ball endmill |
| Passes | Rough only |

## Parameters

Shared settings are documented in [Common operation parameters](/guide/operations/common-parameters/).

| Parameter | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | Chooses Offset, Seeded circles, Parallel, or Trochoidal coverage at each level. |
| **Stepdown** | Sets the vertical distance between roughing levels. |
| **Stock to leave radial** and **Stock to leave axial** | Leave wall or floor material for surface finishing. |
| **Finish walls** and **Finish floor** | Control the optional finishing work on eligible surfaces. |

## Strategies

The roughing patterns are described in [Clearing patterns](/guide/strategies/clearing-patterns/),
[Seeded-circle clearing](/guide/strategies/seeded-circles/), and
[Trochoidal cutting](/guide/strategies/trochoidal-cutting/#clearing). It also supports
[entry and exit moves](/guide/strategies/entry-and-exit/) and [feed reduction](/guide/strategies/feed-reduction/).

## Limitations

The operation protects the model cross-section and retained 2.5D material instead of simply
cutting every horizontal band. Nearby features, regions, clamps, and tabs can therefore split or
omit a pass. Review the warnings and the preview rather than assuming each level is continuous.

## Verifying the result

<Aside type="caution" title="Leave enough material for the finish pass">
	Inspect the stepped result in simulation and confirm that the tool reaches the intended areas
	without gouging protected geometry. A roughing pass is not a final surface finish.
</Aside>
