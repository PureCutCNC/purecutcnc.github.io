Choose an operation from the feature’s role and geometry, then confirm its compatible target,
tool, pass, and strategy. Regions can restrict a compatible cut but are not machining targets on
their own. A 3D finish requires exactly one model; a STEP model counts as a model.

<VisualPending id="operations-choice-map" />

## Requirements

| Goal | Feature target | Operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear a closed internal area | Closed subtract | [Pocket](/guide/operations/pocket/) |
| Face a closed top or model area | Closed add or model | [Surface clean](/guide/operations/surface-clean/) |
| Cut an internal contour | Closed subtract | [Edge route inside](/guide/operations/edge-route-inside/) |
| Cut an outside contour | Closed add or model | [Edge route outside](/guide/operations/edge-route-outside/) |
| Carve closed artwork or a closed line | V-carve-compatible closed geometry | [V-carve offset](/guide/operations/v-carve-offset/) or [V-carve medial](/guide/operations/v-carve-medial/) |
| Follow open or closed artwork | Machinable feature, with optional closed regions | [Engrave](/guide/operations/engrave/) |
| Bore circular holes | Circles, with optional closed regions | [Drill](/guide/operations/drill/) |
