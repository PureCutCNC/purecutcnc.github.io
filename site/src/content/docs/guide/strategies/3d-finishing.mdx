## How it works

3D surface finish offers three patterns. **Parallel** follows repeated scan lines, **Waterline**
follows contour rings around the model, and **Constant scallop** varies the path spacing to hold a
target cusp height on compatible ball-endmill work.

## Settings


<VisualPending id="finishing-patterns" />
| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | Chooses Parallel, Waterline, or Constant scallop. |
| **Scallop height** | Sets the target cusp height for compatible ball-endmill finishing. |
| **Filter by surface slope** | Sets the minimum and maximum surface slopes to machine. |

## Waterline

Waterline can use **Adaptive refinement**. When it is enabled, **Adaptive spacing** and **Max
rings / band** control the refinement of each contour band. Budget warnings mean the requested
ring detail could not all be emitted; inspect the resulting coverage instead of treating the
setting as guaranteed.

## Constant scallop

Constant scallop is a finish pattern for the operation. With a ball endmill, **Scallop height**
sets the target cusp height and the panel shows the implied cusp information. It replaces the
ordinary stepover ratio for that configuration.

## Scallop height


<VisualPending id="finishing-scallop-height" />
Use a smaller scallop height for a finer surface at the cost of more passes. Choose a value that
matches the finish requirement and the tool geometry, then compare the toolpath density in the
preview before committing to machining time.

## Slope filter


<VisualPending id="finishing-slope-filter" />
**Filter by surface slope** limits finishing to the chosen minimum and maximum surface slope. Use
separate operations when steep walls and shallow floors need different strategies or finishes.

## Protection

3D surface operations protect the model cross-section and retained 2.5D material. Nearby features,
regions, clamps, and tabs can interrupt or suppress unsafe moves. This is intentional: review the
warnings and the 3D result for uncovered bands instead of forcing a path through protected material.

## Limitations

<Aside type="danger" title="Safety: a beautiful preview still needs a dry run">
	Finishing paths can be dense and slope-limited. Confirm the model target, tool, workholding,
	clearances, protected areas, and final simulation before exporting or running the job.
</Aside>
