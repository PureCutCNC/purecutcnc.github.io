## How it works

<Screenshot src={entryAndApproach} alt="Seen from the side: plunge drops straight to depth, helix circles down at the ramp angle over its helix diameter, and ramp slopes down and back along the path. Seen from above: the XY approach curves the cut onto the wall on a tangent arc and off it again, where without it the cut simply starts on the wall." />

The **Entry strategy** controls how the tool reaches cutting depth: **Plunge**, **Helix**, or
**Ramp**. A helix or ramp uses its angle setting; a helix also has a diameter setting. When a
strategy cannot fit the geometry, the app reports the fallback in its warnings.

For an outside edge route, entry clearance is checked against the surrounding parts' actual
material, including holes and letter counters. A part enclosed by a ring of other parts can
still use a helix or ramp when there is enough room for that entry. An enclosed position alone
does not require a plunge; inspect the generated entry and any fallback warning, especially
after [nesting](/guide/design/arranging/#nest-on-stock).

## Opening older projects

When you open a project saved before file format 3.2, PureCutCNC resets a stored **Entry
strategy** to **Plunge** on inside and outside edge routes that are not trochoidal. In v0.4,
those routes plunged even if the file recorded another strategy. The load warning counts the
routes reset, and the original file stays unchanged until you save it. Trochoidal edge routes
are not reset; newly created rough edge routes still start with **Helix**.

Check the entry on each affected route and inspect its first cutting move before generating or
exporting the program. See [File formats](/guide/reference/file-formats/#project-format-compatibility)
for the format warning shown by older builds.

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Entry strategy** | Chooses Plunge, Helix, or Ramp. |
| **Ramp angle** | Sets the descent angle for a ramp or helix. |
| **Helix diameter** | Sets the helix size for a helical entry. |

## XY approach

**XY approach & exit** can add a direct or tangent-arc lead-in and lead-out on compatible wall
cuts. It is offered for eligible Pocket, Surface clean, 3D surface rough, and edge-route work.
Edge routes need a ramped entry; trochoidal rough edge routes do not offer this control. A region
mask can also make the lead unavailable.

## Limitations

<Aside type="caution" title="Use the reported fallback">
	The tool cannot always fit a helix, ramp, or tangent lead in the selected geometry. Read the
	warning and inspect the first cutting move; do not assume the requested entry was generated.
</Aside>
