## How it works

**Offset** follows successive inset contours. **Parallel** sweeps straight raster passes at the
selected angle. **Seeded circles** starts from circles placed in the area, then joins the remaining
coverage. Choose the pattern based on the desired finish, path shape, and the feature geometry.

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | Selects Offset, Seeded circles, or Parallel where the operation offers them. |
| **Angle** | Sets the direction of Parallel passes. |
| **Stepover Ratio** | Sets the lateral gap between applicable passes as a fraction of the tool diameter. |

Offset paths simplify compatible polylines into arcs and circles, which can make the generated
G-code smaller and motion smoother.

## Seeded circles

Seeded circles begin from circular seed positions and then clear the remaining material. They are
useful when circular motion suits the geometry, but you should inspect the final links and islands
in the toolpath preview before machining.

## Overlapping subtracts

Non-target subtract features that meet a target can extend the resolved cut region at their own
depth. Long connected chains can reach the resolver's chain limit and report a warning. Inspect
the resolved bands and warnings when nearby pockets overlap; split the geometry or targets if the
result is not the region you intended to clear.

## Limitations

<Aside type="caution" title="A pattern is not available on every operation">
	3D surface finish uses Parallel, Waterline, and Constant scallop instead. Cleanup does not
	offer Trochoidal, Waterline, or Constant scallop.
</Aside>
