## How it works

<Screenshot src={patternComparison} alt="One pocket covered four ways: offset rings following the wall inwards; parallel passes sweeping across and turning at the boundary; circles grown from two seed points with wall-following passes clearing the rest; and a dense coil of trochoidal orbits following the wall with a second run up the middle. The links between passes are drawn dashed." />

**Offset** follows successive inset contours. **Parallel** sweeps straight raster passes at the
selected angle. For circular seeds, see [Seeded-circle clearing](/guide/strategies/seeded-circles/).
For load-managed orbiting paths, see [Trochoidal cutting](/guide/strategies/trochoidal-cutting/#clearing).
Choose the pattern based on the desired finish, path shape, and the feature geometry.

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | Selects the clearing pattern. This page explains Offset and Parallel; Seeded circles and Trochoidal have dedicated guidance. |
| **Angle** | Sets the direction of Parallel passes. |
| **Stepover Ratio** | Sets the lateral gap between applicable passes as a fraction of the tool diameter. |

Offset paths simplify compatible polylines into arcs and circles, which can make the generated
G-code smaller and motion smoother.

## Overlapping subtracts

<Screenshot src={subtractFold} alt="A pocket target, a deeper subtract feature that touches it, and the combined area that is cleared — with a section showing the floor stepping down to the subtract's own depth where the two meet" />

Non-target subtract features that meet a target can extend the resolved cut region at their own
depth. Long connected chains can reach the resolver's chain limit and report a warning. Inspect
the resolved bands and warnings when nearby pockets overlap; split the geometry or targets if the
result is not the region you intended to clear.

## Limitations

<Aside type="caution" title="A pattern is not available on every operation">
	3D surface finish uses Parallel, Waterline, and Constant scallop instead. Cleanup does not
	offer Trochoidal, Waterline, or Constant scallop.
</Aside>
