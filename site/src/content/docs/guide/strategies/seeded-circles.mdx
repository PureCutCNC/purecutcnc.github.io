## How it works

<Screenshot src={seededCircles} alt="Seeded-circle clearing across a large rectangular pocket: concentric rings grow from several circular seeds around open areas, linked by rapid moves" />

**Seeded circles** finds circular regions that fit inside the selected clearing area, grows a stack
of circles from each seed, then uses offset rings for material that remains. It can make the first
passes in open rounded areas more direct than starting every area at its wall.

## Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | Select **Seeded circles** to enable the seed-first clearing plan. |
| **Stepover Ratio** | Sets the lateral gap between the circles and the offset rings that complete coverage. |
| **Round link junctions** | Shapes eligible transitions between the remaining offset rings; see [Linking clearing passes](/guide/strategies/link-junctions/). |

## Limitations

<Aside type="caution" title="Seeds must fit the clearing area">
	A narrow area may have no valid circular seed. In that case the operation falls back to its
	offset-ring coverage; inspect the preview rather than assuming circles were generated.
</Aside>

Separate seed stacks travel at safe Z before the next one begins. Preview the path where several
open areas or islands are involved, especially before machining a part with clamps or other
obstacles.
