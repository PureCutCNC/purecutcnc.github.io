PureCutCNC reports messages where it has enough information to identify a condition worth reviewing. Read the message where it appears, then correct the project or export setting before continuing.

## Operation messages

Operation properties can report geometry, target, tool, depth, clamp, tab, or generation conditions. A warning tells you what to inspect; it does not by itself mean that generation or export is impossible. An error indicates that the operation cannot produce a valid result until corrected.

## Export messages

The **Export G-code** dialog separates **Errors** from **Warnings**. Errors include no selected machine, no selected operation, a missing tool, an out-of-date preview, or an unsupported required capability. Warnings can describe a fallback or a generated-toolpath condition. Read both, but do not imply that every warning blocks export.

## What to do

Check the named operation, tool, machine, and project settings first. Regenerate after an edit, inspect the toolpath and simulation, then reopen export if the preview is out of date. If the message concerns a custom machine, compare the definition with your controller documentation.

| Message concerns | What it means | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target or geometry | The chosen features do not meet the operation's role, shape, or depth requirements. | Read the target hint; check Add/Subtract/Model roles, closed contours, region filters, and Z ranges. |
| Entry or strategy fallback | The requested entry, orbit, or link could not fit the geometry, so a different move may have been generated. | Inspect the first cut and all fallback locations in the preview; change the entry or geometry if necessary. |
| Clamp avoidance | A cut was kept away from recorded workholding, potentially leaving material uncut or skipping a hole. | Check the physical clamp positions, clearances, uncut area, and simulation before machining. |
| Tabs | A tab can block the final depth, miss the route, or overlap another tab. | Check every tab crossing and the final-depth pass, especially after nesting or changing a contour. |
| 3D model or mesh | The model is unreachable, too dense, or protected material limits a pass. | Check the model, tool, region, mesh detail, and the operation's protected area. |
| Machine or post-processor | A requested output feature may be unavailable or expanded into other moves. | Verify canned-cycle, arc, coolant, and tool-change support against the selected machine and inspect the G-code preview. |

The full message names the affected operation or location when available. These categories are a
starting point, not permission to ignore a warning: resolve its specific condition and verify the
resulting motion before cutting.
