The project panel, on the left of the workspace, has two parts: the **Project tree** lists
everything in the project, and **Properties** below it shows the settings of what is selected.
<AppIcon name="expand" /> **Expand properties panel** opens the properties in a larger panel;
<kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

## The tree


<Screenshot
	src={treeSections}
	maxWidth={360}
	alt="Expanded PureCutCNC project tree showing the Project, Grid, Stock, Origin, Backdrop, Features, Regions, Construction, Tabs, and Clamps rows, with five features, four tabs, and two clamps"
	caption="Every tree row, with the bundled PureCutCNC example loaded."
/>
| Row | What it holds | Row actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Project** | Project-wide settings. | None. |
| **Grid** | The sketch grid. | Show or hide. |
| **Stock** | The material the job starts from. | Show or hide. |
| **Origin** | The work zero used for export. | Show or hide. |
| **Backdrop** | A reference image, if the project has one. | Show or hide, once an image is loaded. |
| **Features** | Add, subtract, line, and model features, and their folders. | <AppIcon name="eye" /> **Show all features**, <AppIcon name="eye-off" /> **Hide all features**, <AppIcon name="folder" /> **Add folder**. |
| **Regions** | Region masks and their folders. | <AppIcon name="eye" /> **Show all regions**, <AppIcon name="eye-off" /> **Hide all regions**, <AppIcon name="folder" /> **Add region folder**. |
| **Construction** | Construction geometry and its folders. | <AppIcon name="eye" /> **Show all construction geometry**, <AppIcon name="eye-off" /> **Hide all construction geometry**, <AppIcon name="folder" /> **Add construction folder**. |
| **Tabs** | Tabs for edge routes. | <AppIcon name="eye" /> **Show all tabs**, <AppIcon name="eye-off" /> **Hide all tabs**, **Add tab**, **Select all tabs**. |
| **Clamps** | Clamps the toolpaths must clear. | <AppIcon name="eye" /> **Show all clamps**, <AppIcon name="eye-off" /> **Hide all clamps**, **Add clamp**, **Select all clamps**. |

Each feature row has a role badge (click it to change the role), a <AppIcon name="eye" /> show or
hide button, and a grip for dragging the row (**Drag to reorder**). Badges also mark linked features
(**Linked — N instances share this definition**), include and exclude regions, and construction
geometry (**ref**).

## Properties


<Screenshot
	src={properties}
	alt="Expanded Properties dialog for the feature Rect 2, with a Shape column showing Operation Subtract and an Instance column showing Name, Z top, Z bottom, Folder, Visible, and Locked"
	caption="Expand properties panel opens the same settings in a wider, multi-column view."
/>
What the panel shows depends on the selection.

| Selection | Properties |
| --- | --- |
| **Project** | **Name**; **Units** (**Millimeters** or **Inches**); **Show feature info in sketch**; the clearance heights **Safe Z** and **Op clear Z**; the clamp clearances **Clamp clear XY** and **Clamp clear Z**; **Machine**, with **Manage machines…**. |
| **Grid** | **Grid extent**, **Major lines**, **Minor lines**, **Snap increment**, **Visible**. |
| **Stock** (rectangular) | **Width**, **Height**, **Thickness**, **Color**, **Visible**. |
| **Stock** (from a feature) | **Source feature**, **Thickness**, **Color**, **Visible**, **Edit sketch**, **Reset to rectangle**. |
| **Origin** | **Name**, **Z**, **Visible**, **Place origin**, and the **Presets** **Top left**, **Center top**, and **Bottom left**. |
| **Backdrop** | **Image** (**Load image** or **Replace image**), **Visible**, **Opacity**, **Width**, **Height**, **Angle**, and **Move**, **Resize**, **Rotate**, and **Delete**. |
| A feature | **Shape**: **Operation** (the role), plus **Mask mode** for a region, the text settings for text, and **3D orientation** for a model. **Instance**: **Name**, **Z top** and **Z bottom**, **Folder**, **Visible**, **Locked**. Then **Edit sketch**, **Delete feature**, and any constraints. |
| Several features | The same fields, showing **Mixed values** where the features differ, plus **Group** or **Ungroup** and **Delete selected**. |
| Tabs or clamps | Their size, position, and height, for one or several at a time. See [Tabs](/guide/cam-setup/tabs/) and [Clamps and clearances](/guide/cam-setup/clamps-and-clearances/). |

The **Shape** section holds what linked copies share; when a feature has linked copies, its title
reads **Shape (shared with N instances)**, and **Make unique** appears there. The **Instance**
section holds what belongs to this copy alone. See [Linked features](/guide/fundamentals/linked-features/).

A feature's properties also show a warning when its profile crosses itself (**This profile
self-intersects. 3D/CAM results may be invalid.**) or reaches past the stock (**This profile extends
outside the stock boundary.**).

## Folders and groups

Folders organize the tree. Features, regions, and construction geometry each have their own
folders, and a folder only holds its own kind.

- Create a folder with <AppIcon name="folder" /> **Add folder** (or **Add region folder**,
  **Add construction folder**) on the section row, or choose **Add to folder** > **Create new…** in
  the context menu.
- Move a feature into a folder with **Add to folder**, the **Folder** property, or by dragging it.
- **Select all features in folder** on a folder row selects its contents.

A group is a folder whose members act as one: clicking any member on the canvas or in the tree
selects the whole group, and members cannot be dragged out of it. Select two or more features of
the same kind and choose **Group** (or **Group features** on the folder row); **Ungroup** turns the
group back into a plain folder. Groups do not change any feature's role.

## Visibility

The show or hide button on a row, the section-row actions, and the toggles in the status bar change
an item's visibility. Visibility is saved in the project, and it changes more than what you see:

| Hidden item | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Feature | Not drawn, and left out of the 3D model and of exported models. Operations that target it still generate toolpaths. |
| Tab | Not drawn. Edge routes still leave the tab; 3D surface operations ignore it. |
| Clamp | Not drawn, and **ignored by the clamp clearance and collision checks**. |
| Stock, grid, origin | Not drawn. |

<Aside type="danger" title="Safety: hidden clamps are not avoided">
	Hiding a clamp, including with <AppIcon name="eye-off" /> **Hide all clamps** or the **Clamps**
	status bar toggle, removes it from clearance checking. Toolpaths can then pass through it. Show
	every clamp before you generate and export toolpaths, and check the clearances; see
	[Working safely](/guide/start-here/safety/). This behaviour is tracked in
	[app issue #802](https://github.com/PureCutCNC/purecutcnc/issues/802).
</Aside>

**Locked** protects a feature from accidental changes. While a locked feature is selected, the
transform tools in the tool rail are unavailable; its points cannot be edited; and join, offset,
align, and distribute leave it out.

## Context menu


<Screenshot
	src={contextMenu}
	maxWidth={420}
	alt="Feature context menu open on Rect 1 in the project tree, listing operation, sketch, transform, folder, grouping, cut, use as stock, and delete actions"
	caption="Actions that do not apply to the selection are dimmed."
/>
Right-click a feature on the canvas or in the tree, or long-press it on a touch screen. The menu
offers what applies to the selection:

- **Make unique** and **Select linked instances**, for linked features;
- **Create operation**, **Add to operation**, and **Remove from operation**;
- **Edit sketch** and **Add constraint**;
- **Copy**, **Move**, **Resize**, **Rotate**, **Mirror**, and **Offset** (their names change to
  **Copy selected**, **Move selected**, or **Copy group** and so on for several features or a group);
- **Add to folder**, **Group**, **Join**, and **Cut**;
- **Use as stock**, for a closed add feature;
- **Delete** (**Delete selected**, **Delete group**).

Unavailable items are greyed out, and their tooltip says why, for example **Locked features cannot
be moved** or **Select two or more features to join**.
