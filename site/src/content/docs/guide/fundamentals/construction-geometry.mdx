Construction geometry is drawing reference geometry. It helps you place, constrain, mirror, and
dimension a design without adding material, subtracting material, or becoming a machining target.

## Main idea

Choose the **Construction** creation target before drawing a reference shape, or move existing
reference work into the Construction section when that is appropriate. Construction objects keep
their own section in the feature tree so they cannot be mistaken for normal material features or
region masks.

Construction can support sketching in the same ways that other geometry can: use it as a visual
reference, snap to it, align to it, and dimension against it. It does not have a machining depth
and PureCut CNC excludes it from the model and toolpath targets.

## Typical uses

- Draw a centreline, axis, or symmetry guide before creating the final material features.
- Lay out a repeated pattern, then copy or mirror the finished features against the guide.
- Keep an imported or traced reference visible while the actual add and subtract features remain
  separate.

Hide Construction from the status bar or its tree section when it makes a review harder to read;
that changes display only and does not turn it into material.
