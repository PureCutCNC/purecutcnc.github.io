The PureCutCNC window keeps the design, the machining setup, and the checks in one workspace.
This page names its parts and explains how to move around in it.

## Main idea


<VisualPending id="workspace-layout" />
| Area | Where | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top toolbar | Top | Project, history, zoom, snapping, measuring, appearance, and language controls. |
| Tool rail | Left edge | The drawing, arranging, and editing tools. |
| Project panel | Left | The **Project tree** and the **Properties** of the selection. |
| Centre | Middle | The **Sketch**, **3D view**, and **Simulation** tabs. |
| CAM panel | Right | The **Operations** and **Tools** tabs, with the properties of the selected operation or tool. |
| Status bar | Bottom | Units, stock size, view toggles, and the app version. |

The buttons at either end of the tab row, **Hide project panel** and **Hide CAM panel**, fold the
side panels away to give the centre more room; the same buttons show them again. The divider in
each side panel changes how much height the list and the properties get. On a tablet the side
panels become drawers; see [Tablet and touch](/guide/fundamentals/tablet-and-touch/).

## Views


<VisualPending id="workspace-views" />
Switch views with the tabs above the centre area, or with <kbd>←</kbd>, <kbd>→</kbd>,
<kbd>Home</kbd>, and <kbd>End</kbd> once a tab has focus.

- **Sketch** is the 2D drawing view. Draw and edit features here, and see the toolpaths drawn over
  them.
- **3D view** shows the stock, the solid model built from the features, imported models, and the
  toolpaths in 3D.
- **Simulation** removes material from the stock along the toolpaths, so you can see what the
  job will leave.

Each view answers different questions about the same project, and switching views changes
nothing in it. None of them replaces checking the machine setup and the exported G-code; see
[Working safely](/guide/start-here/safety/).

## Sketch overlays


<VisualPending id="workspace-sketch-overlays" />
Besides your features, the Sketch view shows:

- the stock outline, with its width and height labels (click a label to resize the stock);
- the origin, marked **Origin** with its X and Y axes;
- the grid;
- each feature's name and Z range, when **Feature labels** is on in the status bar (the same
  setting as **Show feature info in sketch** in the **Project** properties);
- tabs and clamps, and the toolpaths of operations whose toolpath is shown.

Features are coloured by role. The **Feature colors** legend on the canvas names the colours:
**Subtract**, **Add feature**, **Region include**, **Region exclude**, **Imported model**, and
**Selected**. Collapse the legend with **Collapse legend**; it then waits in the status bar.

A feature that reaches outside the stock, or whose outline crosses itself, is flagged with a
warning in its properties and while you edit it (**Extends outside stock**, **Self-intersecting
profile**). Clamps that a shown toolpath runs into are highlighted.

## Top toolbar

From left to right:

- the project name (click it to rename the project) and the **Saved** or **Unsaved** badge;
- **New project**, **Open project**, **Import geometry**, **Export model**, **Print design**, and
  **Save project**;
- **Undo** and **Redo**;
- **Zoom to model** and **Zoom selected**;
- the snapping controls: **Enable snapping** or **Disable snapping**, and a button for each snap
  mode;
- the measuring controls: **Tape measure**, **Add dimension**, **Delete dimension**, and
  **Show/hide dimensions**;
- **Appearance** and **Language**.

See [Projects and files](/guide/fundamentals/projects-and-files/),
[Snapping and the grid](/guide/design/snapping-and-grid/),
[Dimensions and constraints](/guide/design/dimensions-and-constraints/),
[Themes and appearance](/guide/reference/themes/), and
[Interface languages](/guide/reference/languages/).

## Tool rail

The tool rail is used in the Sketch view:

- At the top are the four creation targets (**Create features**, **Create lines**, **Create
  regions**, **Create construction geometry**), the shape drawer, and a button for the last shape
  you used. See [Drawing tools](/guide/design/drawing-tools/).
- **Join closed features** and **Cut features** combine and split shapes. See
  [Offset, join, and cut](/guide/design/shape-operations/).
- With features selected, the transform tools appear: copy, move, delete, resize, rotate, mirror,
  and offset, plus **Add constraint** and the **Align selected features** and **Distribute
  selected features** menus. See [Selecting and transforming](/guide/design/selecting-and-transforming/)
  and [Aligning and distributing](/guide/design/arranging/).
- With a backdrop image selected, its move, resize, rotate, and delete tools appear instead. See
  [Backdrop images](/guide/design/backdrop-images/).

The point-editing tools are not in the rail: they are in the **Edit sketch** panel that opens when
you edit a feature. See [Editing sketches](/guide/design/sketch-editing/).

## Status bar

From left to right, the status bar shows:

- the project name;
- the project units (for example **INCH**): click to change them, see
  [Stock, origin, and units](/guide/fundamentals/stock-origin-units/#units);
- the stock size (**Stock: 4 × 3 × 0.75 inch**);
- the **View visibility** toggles: **Feature labels**, **Grid**, **Stock**, **Backdrop**,
  **Origin**, **Regions**, **Construction**, **Tabs**, and **Clamps** (a toggle is unavailable when
  the project has none of that item);
- the collapsed feature colour legend, when you have collapsed it;
- in the web app, the version (**PureCutCNC** and the version number), which opens
  **About PureCutCNC**.

These settings are saved with the project. Most only change what is drawn, but hiding clamps also
removes them from clamp checking; see
[Feature tree and properties](/guide/fundamentals/feature-tree/#visibility).

## Workflow panels


<VisualPending id="workspace-workflow-panel" />
Commands that need several steps, such as drawing a shape, moving features, or joining them, show a
workflow panel on the canvas. Its title names the command, a line below says what to do next (for
example **Click opposite corner or enter dimensions**), and its buttons offer the choices for that
step. Drag the panel by its handle if it covers the part of the sketch you need.

The panel's buttons show their keys:

- <kbd>Tab</kbd> opens numeric entry, such as **Dimensions**, **Distance**, **Scale**, or **Angle**;
- <kbd>Enter</kbd> confirms, or finishes an open path;
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the step or the command;
- <kbd>Backspace</kbd> removes the last point while drawing a polygon, spline, or composite path;
- <kbd>L</kbd>, <kbd>A</kbd>, and <kbd>S</kbd> switch a composite path's next segment to a line, an
  arc, or a spline;
- <kbd>K</kbd> turns **Keep originals** on or off.

All keys are listed on [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/).

## Undo and redo

**Undo** and **Redo** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>,
or <kbd>Cmd</kbd> on macOS) step through changes to the project, such as drawing and editing
features, changing properties, and adding operations or tools. A multi-step command such as a move
is one undo step.

They do not cover application preferences, which are not part of the project: the appearance,
the interface language, and your machine library. Zooming and panning are not undoable either.

## Navigation

In the Sketch view:

| Action | Mouse and keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Pan | Drag with the middle or right button, or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+drag. |
| Zoom | Scroll; the view zooms around the pointer. |
| Frame the whole project | **Zoom to model**. |
| Zoom to an area | **Zoom selected**, then drag a box around the area. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels. |
| Select | Click a feature, or drag a box on empty canvas. See [Selecting and transforming](/guide/design/selecting-and-transforming/). |
| Edit a feature's points | Double-click it. Double-clicking a tab or clamp edits it. |

In the 3D view and the simulation:

| Action | Mouse and keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Orbit | Drag with the left button. |
| Pan | Drag with the middle or right button, or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+drag. |
| Zoom | Scroll. |
| Standard views | The camera menu: **Top view**, **Bottom view**, **Front view**, **Back view**, **Right view**, **Left view**, **Isometric view**, **Fit to model**, and **Reset view**. |

Touch gestures are on [Tablet and touch](/guide/fundamentals/tablet-and-touch/).
