PureCut CNC keeps drawing, model inspection, machining setup, and project controls in one
workspace. The active view stays in the centre; the surrounding panels give you the controls
for the part of the job you are doing.

## Main idea

The workspace has four practical areas:

- The top toolbar holds project, history, and viewing commands.
- The centre holds the current workspace view.
- The project and CAM panels hold the part, setup, and machining information.
- The tool rail and workflow panels provide the drawing and editing commands.

On a desktop-sized screen, pull-out handles open the project and CAM panels without changing the
current view. On a tablet, the same controls are presented in drawers so the canvas remains the
main working area. See [Tablet and touch](/guide/fundamentals/tablet-and-touch/) for the tablet
layout and gestures.

## Views

Use the centre tabs to move between **Sketch**, **3D view**, and **Simulation**. The tab list
supports the arrow keys as well as a pointer.

- **Sketch** is where you draw, select, and edit features. It is the usual place to set up a
  project.
- **3D view** is where you inspect the stock, the solid model, and the toolpaths in three
  dimensions.
- **Simulation** shows the result of removing material from the stock. Use it to inspect the
  result of a selected toolpath before exporting G-code.

The views answer different questions about the same project. A 3D view or simulation is useful
for checking a result, but it does not replace checking the workholding, machine setup, and
exported G-code before a cut.

## Sketch overlays

The Sketch view shows the stock outline and origin axes as well as your features. Its feature
colours distinguish add and subtract material, regions, imported models, and the current
selection. Expand the **Feature colors** legend from the status bar when you need to identify a
colour.

Warnings on the canvas call out a profile that extends outside the stock or self-intersects.
Those warnings are signals to correct the design before relying on a model or toolpath. Clamp
and toolpath-collision display belongs to the machining views; see [Clamps and
clearances](/guide/cam-setup/clamps-and-clearances/) when that page is available.

## Top toolbar

The top toolbar keeps everyday project actions together: **New project**, **Open project**,
**Import geometry**, **Export model**, **Print design**, and **Save project**. It also contains
**Undo**, **Redo**, **Zoom to model**, and **Zoom selected**.

The current project name reports whether it is saved or has unsaved changes. The bar also gives
you the snap settings and the measurement tools, plus the appearance and interface-language
controls. Use [Projects and files](/guide/fundamentals/projects-and-files/) for opening and
saving, and [Keyboard shortcuts](/guide/reference/keyboard-shortcuts/) for their shortcuts.

## Tool rail

The tool rail is the starting point for drawing and arranging in the Sketch view. Choose the
creation target first, then choose a shape from the drawer. A target can create a normal feature,
a line, a region, or construction geometry; the available shapes follow that choice.

Its other groups contain the commands for selection and arrangement, including copy, move,
delete, resize, rotate, mirror, offset, constraints, join, cut, alignment, and distribution.
Some commands appear only when the current selection makes them valid. The Edit Sketch panel
holds the controls for changing the nodes and segments of an existing sketch.

## Status bar

The status bar shows the stock dimensions and lets you change project units. Expand it for the
**View visibility** controls: feature labels, grid, stock, backdrop, origin, regions,
construction geometry, tabs, and clamps can each be shown or hidden without changing the project.

It also opens **About PureCutCNC** and the expanded feature-colour legend. Hiding an item only
changes what you see in the workspace; it does not delete or change the project data.

## Workflow panels

Many canvas commands are staged: you may choose a feature, pick points, enter a value, then
confirm the result. PureCut CNC shows the active command in a movable workflow panel. Follow the
step shown in that panel rather than trying to complete the whole command in one click.

The available keyboard help depends on the active command. <kbd>Tab</kbd> opens numeric entry
where a workflow offers it, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> completes an eligible shape or command.
<kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the current step or command. While drawing a polygon, spline, or composite
path, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> removes the last point or segment; in a composite path,
<kbd>L</kbd>, <kbd>A</kbd>, and <kbd>S</kbd> choose line, arc, and spline segments. In the Join,
Cut, and applicable transform panels, <kbd>K</kbd> toggles whether to keep the originals.

## Undo and redo

**Undo** and **Redo** track edits to the project, including drawing and editing features. They do
not undo application-wide preferences such as the selected interface language or appearance, and
they do not undo changes to the machine library. Save a project when a meaningful milestone is
complete so you have a file you can return to.

## Navigation

In the Sketch view, pan with middle-button drag, right-button drag, or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+drag.
Scroll to zoom at the pointer location. **Zoom to model** frames the project, while **Zoom
selected** lets you draw a window around the area to inspect. Double-click an editable feature to
enter its sketch-editing mode.
